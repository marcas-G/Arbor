## Acceptance Evidence

- `ExecutionSettlement` is a discriminated union; exhaustive switch with
  `never` default. A type-level `@ts-expect-error` proves a `Completed`
  without a `CompletedResult` cannot be built (no arbitrary outcome×result).
- Admission fixes `sessionId`; `WorkspaceExecution` admission with
  `noOtherActiveMain = false` is rejected with `ActiveExecutionConflict`.
- `ExecutionBoundAgentBinding` is consumed from P0-015 (not redefined).
- `stopExecution` records a stop-request fact and keeps the Execution
  `Active`; `SettleExecution` is once-only (`TerminalLifecycleMutation` on a
  second settle); a settled Execution cannot be stopped.
- `CompletionClaimed` carries `workRevision` + `claimRef` for deterministic
  replay.
- No lifecycle states beyond `Active` / `Settled`; lease is not a lifecycle
  state.
- No infrastructure import in the domain package.

## Deviations

1. The `noOtherActiveMain` precondition is applied to `WorkspaceExecution`
   bindings (the design scopes the active-main uniqueness to Workspace main
   executions); execution-bound agent admissions are unaffected.
