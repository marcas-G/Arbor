## Acceptance Evidence

- Claim write inserts into `resource_ownership`; `listActiveByWorkspace` returns
  it with `releasedAt = null`.
- Overlapping active claim (parent/child path) rejects with
  `ResourceOwnershipRepositoryFailure`; after `releaseClaim`
  (`UPDATE ... WHERE released_at IS NULL`) a new overlapping claim is allowed.
- `environment_revisions` lazily initialized; absent row reads as `Option.none`;
  a moved revision fails `ResourceResolutionStale` before any write.
- `resolve` maps FileTree/GitWorktree/DatabaseNamespace/ExternalResource to
  `{resourceSpaceId, normalizedRegion}` and yields a deterministic revision.
