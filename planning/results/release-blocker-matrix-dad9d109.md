## Decision rules

`Closed` applies only to the named contract/module and cited source baseline;
`Partial` means some bounded implementation or evidence exists but the module
or scenario is not fully qualified; `Open` means an accepted design still has
no implementation/authorization, or a required decision/qualification is
missing. Historical green evidence is never promoted to this candidate unless
its commit is an ancestor and its stated scope matches. A targeted result does
not stand in for `pnpm check` or `pnpm test:functional`.

The product objective remains the frozen Problem & Goals v1.3 and Scenarios
v1.3 (`docs/design/00-problem-goals.md`, `docs/design/01-scenarios.md`):
stable responsibility and continuity (G1–G8, P1–P8) with high-level human
control, independent verification and acceptance, and safe long-running
recovery. The scenario flow is not equivalent to a feature checklist: every
Closed item below is scoped to its stated contract.
