## Goal

Replace HumanMessage-driven unbounded retry with a durable ResponseJob/Attempt
runtime; block causally incomplete context before Provider bytes; classify and
bound recovery; materialize exact purpose-specific turn tools; expose
attention/retry/cancel state to humans.
