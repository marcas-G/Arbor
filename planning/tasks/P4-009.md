# P4-009 — `SandboxPort` + local adapter

## Source

- P4 `04`; DID v1.8 G3; SD v1.3 §6.5

## Depends On

- P4-002

## Objective

Implement the SandboxPort contract and a minimal local executor.

## Outputs

- `SandboxPort` + `adapters/sandbox-local`.

## Must Hold

- Isolation scoped to admitted regions; cannot reach the control DB or raw secrets.
- `close` releases resources.

## Must Not Decide

- No worktree/git isolation (P11).
- No authority decisions.

## Acceptance

- open/close lifecycle; writes confined to writable regions.
