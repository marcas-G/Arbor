# P5-005 — Multi-turn Session continuity

## Source

- P5 `04` §1; SD v1.3 §5.4

## Depends On

- P5-002
- P5-003
- P5-004

## Objective

Prove Session continuity across Executions on a fixed `execution.sessionId`.

## Outputs

- End-to-end flow: Execution 1 appends ModelOutput/Observation; a later Execution reuses the same `primarySessionId`.

## Must Hold

- `execution.sessionId` immutable; primarySessionId snapshot.
- `Session History != Provider Context`; prepareTurn projects.

## Must Not Decide

- No session replacement inside an active main.
- No history replay as context.

## Acceptance

- Two Executions share the same session; entries survive.

pnpm test p5-session-continuity
pnpm check
