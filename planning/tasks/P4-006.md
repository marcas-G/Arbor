# P4-006 — Trusted `InvocationAuthority` + capability ceiling

## Source

- P4 `03` §1–§3; DID v1.8 G1; SD v1.3 §8.1–§8.3

## Depends On

- P4-004
- P4-005

## Objective

Implement deterministic exact-match + capability-ceiling checks over a trusted fact.

## Outputs

- `checkInvocationAuthority(authority, intent, context, regions)` → Ok|Denied.

## Must Hold

- Exact-match on principal/workspace/execution/tool/version/regions/controlBasisDigest/expiry/delegationDepth.
- No PermissionGrant/Parent/User resolution.
- Delegation cannot amplify authority.

## Must Not Decide

- No default-allow.
- No policy computation from governance facts.

## Acceptance

- Every mismatch → `Denied`; matching fact → Ok.
