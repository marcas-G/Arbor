  `application → domain, ports`; `adapters/* → domain, ports`;
  no deep imports; no catch-all error / service locator.

- `domain` remains dependency-free.

## Must Not Decide

- No package-level behavior.
- No weakening of the matrix.

- Synthetic-manifest tests still fail on forbidden edges/unknown packages.
- `domain` proven dependency-free.
