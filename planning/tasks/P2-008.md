# P2-008 — `SessionRepository.appendEntry` + `AgentExecutionState` store

## Source

- P2 `02` §3, `04` §3.3–§3.4; DID v1.7 §1.7, §6.3, §9.8, §12.11

## Depends On

- P2-004

## Objective

Implement Session entry append (with optional fence) and the AgentExecutionState store.

## Outputs

- `SessionRepository.appendEntry` with Session-local sequence.
- `AgentExecutionStateStore` (upsert/find) per `04` §3.3.

## Must Hold

- `sequence = MAX(sequence)+1` serialized by `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`.
- When `fence` is present, the authoritative generation check shares the append transaction; mismatch → `LeaseFencingRejected`.
- Entry kinds limited to `Input | ModelOutput | Observation | CheckpointReference | ContextUpdate`.
- Streaming deltas are never stored.

## Must Not Decide

- No compaction behavior (P3).
- No business lifecycle on Session.

## Acceptance

- Sequences are contiguous and monotonic per Session.
- Append with a stale generation fails without writing.
- AgentExecutionState round-trips the §3.7 fields.

pnpm test p2-session-append
pnpm check
